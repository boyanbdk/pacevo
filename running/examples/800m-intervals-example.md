# 800m Intervals Example

## Original Workout

- Warm-up: 1.6 km at conversational pace.
- Rest: 90 sec walking rest.
- Main set: 8 x 800 m at 4:00/km, range 3:50-4:10/km.
- Rest after each rep: 90 sec walking rest.
- Cool down: 1 km at conversational pace or slower.

## User Inputs

- Treadmill mode: distance-based.
- Easy/warm-up pace: 6:30/km.
- Cool-down pace: 7:00/km.
- Rest walking pace: 4.5 km/h.
- Feeling: 2/10.
- Desired push: normal.

## Adjustment Logic

Feeling is very low, so normal downshifts to easy-normal. The workout still keeps the 800 m structure, but the paces stay mostly on the slower side of the prescribed 3:50-4:10/km window. The first rep starts easier at 4:20/km, most reps sit at 4:10/km, and only the final rep reaches the target 4:00/km.
